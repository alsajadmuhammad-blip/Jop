---
name: Public interface direction
description: Durable visual and content direction for the public homepage, job cards, and job detail pages.
---

The public-facing experience should feel premium, calm, and practical. Use short Arabic copy, clear information hierarchy, restrained color and decoration, and make the next action obvious. Job cards should prioritize title, company, location, work type, salary, and publication date. Job details should separate the job facts, description, requirements, and contact actions into readable sections.

**Why:** Earlier public layouts felt cluttered and promotional, with cartoon-like card treatments and too much explanatory copy. The product is a job marketplace, so users need to scan and decide quickly.

**How to apply:** Prefer strong layout and spacing over extra slogans, decorative gradients, or empty placeholder sections. Keep empty states concise and avoid showing sections that have no useful content.
