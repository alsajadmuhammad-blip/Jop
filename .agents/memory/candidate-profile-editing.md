---
name: Candidate profile editing
description: Expected behavior for job seekers editing education and work experience in their professional profile.
---

Job seekers expect their professional profile to save automatically while they edit. Education should show the qualification, specialization, and graduation year; work experience should show a start and end period.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested these as expected candidate-profile behaviors.

**How to apply:** keep the candidate editor, employer-facing profile, and resume exports consistent when changing these profile details.
